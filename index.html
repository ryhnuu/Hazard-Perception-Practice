How it works

1. Mix it up

Practice gives you a random mix of hazard clips, theory questions and road sign questions, like the real test. Use Hazards or Practice Theory to focus on one topic.

2. Press Space

Flag the hazard as soon as you think it will make you slow down or change direction. Earlier means more points, up to 5.

3. Learn

See your reaction time against the scoring window. If you miss full marks, an animated replay shows when to flag.

The golden rules

A developing hazard is something that makes you change speed or direction. Look for the clue first, press as soon as it starts to develop, press once or twice only, and do not press for things that stay put.
