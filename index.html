🚦 Hazard Ready

Spot the hazard before it becomes one.

Practise hazard perception the way the real driving theory test does it: watch the road from the driver's seat, press Space the moment a hazard starts to develop, and see exactly how early you reacted.

How it works

1. Watch

Four clips in different light and weather. Most contain one developing hazard. One is a decoy with none.

2. Press Space

Flag the hazard as soon as you think it will make you slow down or change direction. Earlier means more points, up to 5.

3. Learn

See your reaction time against the scoring window. If you miss full marks, an animated replay shows when to flag.

Also included: theory questions, a road signs trainer and a profile with streaks and badges. This is a practice tool, not an official DVSA product.
